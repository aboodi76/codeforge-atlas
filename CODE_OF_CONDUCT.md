# CodeForge Atlas Community Standards

CodeForge Atlas is intended to be a constructive, inclusive engineering project. We expect participants to communicate professionally, welcome good-faith questions, respect differing technical views, and protect confidential information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, or knowingly unsafe contributions are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove content and may limit participation when necessary to protect the community or the project.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers with relevant context. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible. This document is a project policy and will be revised as the community grows.
