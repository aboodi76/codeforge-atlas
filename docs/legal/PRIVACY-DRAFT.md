# Draft Privacy Notice — Legal Review Required

> **Draft only.** This document is a product-design starting point and must be reviewed by qualified counsel for the selected launch jurisdiction before public use.

CodeForge Atlas is designed to process the account information required for authentication, the source code and change-intent text a user deliberately submits, generated reports, and session messages. The project should document the exact service providers, hosting regions, retention periods, legal basis for processing, user rights, subprocessors, and contact details before launch.

The intended product posture is private-by-default sessions, no execution of submitted source, and no intentional sale of submitted code. Final policy language must define operational retention/deletion behavior and legal rights based on **[JURISDICTION]**.
